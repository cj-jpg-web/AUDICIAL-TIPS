04 / One event. Three reporting directions.

Start with the same verified result: Falcons 68, Tigers 66, after trailing 42–54 at the end of the third. Each direction requires its own supporting evidence.

Sports news / result + turning point

Falcons overturn 12-point gap to edge Tigers

The Falcons outscored the Tigers 26–12 in the final quarter to secure a 68–66 championship victory.

Gather: official score, decisive sequence, player names, key statistics, and post-game reaction.

Sports feature / human experience

Before the comeback, the early mornings

Possible angle: explore the preparation behind the team’s late-game stamina. This needs interviews and observed details; the score alone cannot establish it.

Gather: a player’s routine, specific training scenes, setbacks, and corroborating coach interviews.

Sports editorial / argued position

Every team deserves a safe place to train

Possible position: argue for equitable access to safe training facilities, if the press-conference material provides evidence of the problem.

Gather: facility conditions, access records, affected perspectives, policy, and feasible remedies.

Framework ≠ prewritten story. The game supports the result. It does not automatically prove a training routine or a facilities problem. Report those angles before using them.

05 / Keep the action and its context.

This schematic stands in for a reporting photo so you can inspect composition. A tighter crop is only useful when it preserves what readers need to understand.

Caption practice: identify the person, describe the verified action, and give event context and credit. Never invent names, a decisive-shot claim, or timing from appearance alone.

Full sample / fictional training article

Falcons overturn 12-point gap to edge Tigers

By Training Desk · Fictional practice material

The Falcons outscored the Tigers 26–12 in the fourth quarter to complete a comeback and claim a 68–66 championship victory.

Trailing 42–54 after three quarters, the Falcons reversed a game the Tigers had led at every previous quarter break. The Tigers opened with a 20–14 first quarter and extended their advantage to 38–30 at halftime.

The Falcons’ 14-point fourth-quarter advantage erased the 12-point deficit and left them two points ahead at the final buzzer.

What a real report still needs: date and venue, verified player names, the decisive play, attributed quotes, and official competition context. This short sample uses only the fictional box score above.
