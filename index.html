When the moment
becomes the story

A large photo gives the moment room. A smaller photo leaves more room for the text. Choose according to the story’s importance and the quality of your actual image.

Above: let the image lead.

Useful for a lead story with a strong horizontal photo. Readers see the moment before entering the article.

Always add: a verified caption and photo credit. Do not claim the photo shows a winning shot or decisive moment unless your reporting confirms it.

06
Use an infographic

Pick the graphic
that explains the facts.

Do not add a chart just to decorate. Choose one that makes the information quicker to understand.

Choose a scorecard: put the team names and final scores together.

Always include: a clear title, readable labels, units when needed, and the source. In WordPress, upload an accurate graphic as an image with alt text, or build a simple fact box with native blocks.

08
Share the work

One team.
More than one pair of hands.

Everyone should know the basics. That keeps the layout artist from doing every small task alone.

A SIMPLE HANDOFF

Write → check → place → test

  • Writer: finish the draft and suggest a headline.
  • Editor: check facts, grammar, and the angle.
  • Available teammate: add the text, photo, and caption.
  • Layout lead: check consistency across sections.
  • Another teammate: open the published links.

Assign one owner per page. Coordinate before two people edit the same page.

ONE EVENT, DIFFERENT ANGLES

Decide what the piece will do.

News · explain the resultHow did the team turn the game around?
Feature · explore a personWhat experience shaped one player’s journey?
Editorial · argue a positionWhat sports issue deserves a supported recommendation?

Practice the frameworks. Let actual facts and interviews decide the final angle. Do not invent a background story to fit a template.

Practice drill: swap roles for one short exercise. Each member adds a headline, article, photo, caption, and category, then previews the result.

Full article / fictional practice sample
Sports news

Falcons overturn 12-point deficit to win final

By Practice Desk · Fictional sample

The Falcons outscored the Tigers 26–12 in the fourth quarter to complete a comeback and win the championship final, 68–66.

Trailing 42–54 after three quarters, the Falcons reversed a game the Tigers had led at each previous quarter break. The Tigers opened with a 20–14 first quarter and led 38–30 at halftime.

The Falcons’ 14-point advantage in the last quarter erased the deficit and left them two points ahead at the final buzzer.

A real report still needs: verified player names, date and venue, the decisive sequence, and attributed interviews. This short practice article uses only fictional box-score data.
